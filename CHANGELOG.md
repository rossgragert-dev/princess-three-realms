# Changelog
## 0.4.0d.5 "Infinite Adventures" (preview)
- **Puzzle library:** seven families (letter sounds, rhymes, counting, shape matching, patterns, story sequences, nature/garden), each with 10+ authored picture-first challenges in three tiers (sound 36, rhyme 30, counting 20, shape 36, pattern 12, story 10, nature 30). Challenges shuffle with no immediate repeats (recent ids are saved), difficulty adapts gently (two clean wins step up; hints or several tries step down), and replays never re-award stickers or decor. Pip's menu offers all seven families as pictures; Pip, the tree, the shell, the garden plot and the rainbow bridge offer extra rounds after their story step. Nothing is ever locked.
- **River race rebuilt as a scrolling 2D run:** the mermaid stays near the bottom of the screen while the river scrolls toward her; steer left/right with a thumb (or arrow keys), pass through glowing rings and dodge kelp and rocks. A dolphin swims ahead toward the next ring. Free Swim (endless, pearls), Friendly Race (8 rings) and Star Challenge (14 rings). No time penalties and no clock on screen: bumps only slow her briefly, stars come from rings collected. Big exit button top-left at all times; replay or leave buttons at the finish. Progress is recorded; leaving mid-run loses nothing.
- Tests: `puzzleLibrary.test.ts`, `riverRun.test.ts` (366 unit tests), real-Chrome drivers `v04d-library.mjs`, `v04d-race.mjs`.
- Known: the older `v04c-river.mjs` driver's race section targets the retired top-down race and is superseded by `v04d-race.mjs`; lantern-game redesign still pending.

## 0.4.0d.4 "Gardens and Enchantment" (preview)
- **Free garden design:** a decorate button now appears in the Royal Magic Garden. Pick from 24 picture pieces in four tabs (flowers, trees and bushes, structures, ornaments), tap or drag to aim (green = fits, red = try elsewhere), press the check; tap a placed piece to move it or put it away; undo. Three open lawns (a lawn button hops between them). Solid pieces can never wall anything off: every garden object and both entrances stay reachable. Designs are saved by stable id with strict bounds, overlap and quantity checks; old saves load unchanged.
- **Greenhouse of Seeds:** three picture shelves (seed packets, potted trees and bushes, ornaments). Choose one to carry out to the garden (it floats beside her), then tap open grass to plant it; a little rain falls and it blooms. A one-time spoken/picture lesson (choose, plant, water) shows the first time.
- **Enchanted Woods** are brighter: the night tint is much lighter and the glow lights are softer.
- Tests: `garden.test.ts` (350 unit tests total), real-Chrome driver `v04d-garden.mjs`.

## 0.4.0d.3 "Bedroom and Companions" (preview)
- **Princess beds redrawn:** Cozy, Sunny and Canopy beds now have a tall starred headboard, two pillows, a patterned folded blanket and footboard/posts; the Canopy bed adds drapes and a canopy top. Furniture rearranging is unchanged.
- **Sleep:** tap the bed (wherever it is placed) and the princess tucks in, the room darkens under a moon and twinkling stars with floating Zs, fades to black, then warm morning sunbeams bring her back beside the bed. Repeatable, no clock, no penalties; reduced-effects mode uses calmer visuals.

- **Pet in the bedroom:** the chosen pet lives in the room with a two-frame idle and a gentle wander, never blocks doors or furniture, hides while decorating, and hops with hearts when tapped. Saved in the game save; old saves load unchanged.
- Tests: 340+ unit tests (`bedroomPet.test.ts`), real-Chrome driver `v04d-bedroom.mjs`.

## 0.4.0c "The Kingdom Beyond" (preview)
- **Kingdom's Lost Magic:** after meeting Willa, the King or Queen explains the kingdom's magic is getting sleepy (one short optional beat, `KINGDOM_TOLD`). Three new places wake it up. Chapter 1 and every earlier area stay fully playable; nothing here is required and nothing is timed (except the optional Star Challenge).
- **Enchanted Woods** (south trail from the Whispering Woods, after Willa): a twilight forest with ancient faced trees, glowing mushrooms, hanging lamps, woodland animals, a winding path, a stream and secret nooks. *Whispering Trees* (letter sound -> pick the picture, S/C/F), *Rhyme Bridge* (match rhyming pictures: cat/hat, bee/tree, frog/log) which rebuilds the plank bridge over the stream, *Magic Lanterns* (watch the glowing lanterns, tap them in the same order; a wrong tap only replays the pattern) which opens the hidden clearing, and *Storybook Stumps* (put 3 picture cards in beginning/middle/end order). All replayable.
- **Royal Magic Garden** (east of the courtyard, and through the Queen's conservatory glass door): flower beds, ponds, fountain, butterfly garden, hedge paths, greenhouse, benches, archways, friendly animals. *Growing Magic* (pick a seed colour, plant, water, watch it sprout and bloom in that colour; replant any bed in another colour), the fountain wakes once three flowers have grown (touch it again to change the spray colour), *Garden Counting* (3 ladybugs), *Garden Sequencing* (seed, water, flower), and a hedge *Butterfly Maze* (butterflies follow you; lead each to the flower of its colour; a different flower just makes it flutter). Grown flowers and the fountain colour are saved; held seeds and spell effects are not.
- **Shimmering River** (a new whirlpool on the shore, mermaid only): a flowing river with lily pads, reeds, fish, waterfalls, bridges overhead and a hidden alcove behind the kelp. *Mermaid River Race* with rings that follow the current: Free Swim (no rings, no clock), Friendly Race (6 checkpoints with a dolphin friend) and Star Challenge (8 checkpoints, optional timer, 1-3 stars). Only the next ring counts and missing one never costs anything; a big exit button is always on screen; leaving loses nothing. Bubble magic chimes shells (four different shells wake the Musical Pearls), spins water wheels and parts the kelp.
- **Environmental magic system** (`world/envMagic.ts` + `scenes/kingdomLayer.ts`): one target shape, per-form capability rules and bounded temporary effects (at most 24 at once). Sleeping flowers bloom, lanterns light, musical mushrooms play a note, vines uncurl to reveal a passage for good (flag-driven gate), butterflies follow briefly, water wheels spin, shells chime, fountains change spray colour, ancient trees speak, hidden treasure sparkles. The princess wand, unicorn horn and mermaid bubbles all reach these targets in a straight line.
- **Rewards** (granted once, saved, never required): 14 new stickers, plus bedroom decorations Woodland Plush, Mushroom Lamp, Lantern Garland, Flower Cushion and River Fountain (new runtime-drawn art). Saves gain a sanitized `kingdom` block (garden, fountain colour, finished activities, race records); old saves load unchanged; the river always puts her in mermaid form.
- New scene ids `enchanted`, `garden`, `river` with safe spawns, guide exits, picture glyphs and ~85 optional narration clips (docs regenerated).
- Tests: 294 unit tests (was 264) incl. `kingdom.test.ts` (env rules, maze connectivity, ring course in swimmable water, race logic, reward once-only, save sanitation); real-Chrome drivers `v04c-smoke/woods/garden/river/viewports.mjs`.

## 0.4.0b.2 "Full-Screen iPad" (preview)
- **Responsive display:** the fixed 512x384 FIT canvas is replaced by `Phaser.Scale.EXPAND`. The authored 512x384 area is the guaranteed-visible minimum; the game grows along whichever axis the screen is longer, so the canvas fills the whole viewport with no side bars, no stretching and the same crisp integer-less nearest-neighbour pixels. Gameplay coordinates, collision and maps are unchanged.
- **World:** the camera simply sees more map on wide screens (and more vertically in portrait); bounds follow the real camera size, are re-applied on rotation/resize without moving the player, and maps smaller than the screen are centred on the map colour. Bedroom decorating centres the room above the bottom sheet on any shape.
- **HUD:** joystick, A, wand/magic, form tray, home/book/decorate, hint, goal bubble and star counter are anchored to the real left/right/top/bottom edges and re-laid out on resize. Dialogue sits on the bottom edge; puzzles, reveals, counting tray and tag panel are centred. Touch targets keep their size.
- **Menus and minigames** (Title, Wardrobe, Vanity, Room Designer, Fireworks, Wand Workshop, Stickerbook, Parent Settings, station screens, Boot): 512x384 stage is centred with full-bleed backdrops and dimmers; pointer positions are converted to stage coordinates.
- **Home Screen:** manifest `display: standalone`, `orientation: any`; rotation nudges the scale manager so the canvas refits.
- Tests: new `src/tests/stage.test.ts` (responsive sizing, stage fit, HUD bounds); `tools/local-verify/viewports.mjs` runs five viewports in real Chrome (coverage, no stretch, HUD bounds, tap-to-move).

## 0.4.0b "Family Magic & Unicorn Meadow" (preview)
- **Wand fix (first):** the princess's wand no longer auto-aims. The projectile spawns, tweens and collides along the single direction she visibly faces (up/down/left/right). Only a direct hit activates a target; objects beside the line are passed. Sparkle visuals, reactions, cooldown (420 ms), range (6 tiles) and the two-thumb use are unchanged. Unit tests prove nearby off-axis targets cannot redirect a shot; a real-Chrome driver shoots straight past a tree.
- **King's Fireworks Festival:** new Festival Garden west of the courtyard. The King and a launch dais open a night-sky screen with the castle silhouette. 6 colours, 5 designs (star, heart, flower, spiral, rainbow) and 1/2/3 rockets in a row; tap the sky to launch. Unlimited practice, no timers. No strobe or full-screen flash; at most 3 bursts and 96 particles each (reduced effects: slower, 40 particles). First completed session (3 launches) gives the Festival Star Garland decoration, once.
- **Queen's Wand Workshop:** new Conservatory off the courtyard. A dedicated workshop screen with an animated preview: 6 wand heads (star, moon, flower, heart, crystal, seashell), 8 colours (adds mint, red, pearl, rainbow), 4 magical trails (star sparkles, butterflies, flower petals, bubbles). 3 heads, 6 colours and 2 trails are open from the start; the rest are earned by exploring (fireworks, tag, the shore, the first star, the rainbow bridge, Marina). Locked choices show a dark shape, a padlock and a picture of where they come from. The equipped wand is saved (`equippedWand`, `unlockedWand`) and shown in the projectile and its trail during normal play. Cosmetic only: aim, range and collision never change.
- **Unicorn Tag:** Unicorn Meadow, reached by a path north of the Whispering Woods once Willa is met. Dapple is a friendly unicorn who plays tag for 3 rounds with a star tally; she is slower than the player, pauses to catch her breath, never traps the child, invites the child to play if they stop, and gives up gracefully. No failure state, no timer, exit at any time. The first completed 3-tag session unlocks the Unicorn Flower Cushion. Pure AI module `src/world/tag.ts` with unit tests.
- Saves: new `equippedWand`, `unlockedWand`, `activities` fields with sanitizers; old saves migrate with the default wand. New SceneIds `festival`, `conservatory`, `meadow` have safe spawns and exits.
- New original pixel art (generated deterministically by `tools/art_family.py`): King, Queen, Dapple, 48 wand heads, 8 projectile colours, trail sprites, firework icons, launch dais, castle silhouette, wand bench, garland, cushion, heart.
- Tests: 218 unit tests (was 181), new `tests/e2e/family.spec.ts`, and real-Chrome drivers `tools/local-verify/v04b.mjs` and `wand.mjs`.
