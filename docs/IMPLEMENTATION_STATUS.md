# Implementation status - v0.3 Royal Wardrobe & Enchanted Vanity: implemented and browser-tested on the preview; details in `docs/ROYAL_WARDROBE_IMPLEMENTATION.md`.

(Earlier status below: v0.2) "Picture-first + Bedroom Rewards"

Two design changes on top of the standalone v0.1b build (details: `docs/PICTURE_FIRST.md`, `docs/DECORATING.md`):
1. **Narration is no longer a dependency.** New saves start with narration OFF; the child flow is pictures only. Audio code, manifest, tools and the 81 temporary clips remain and work when a grown-up turns narration on.
2. **The bedroom is a reward system:** 12 hotspots in 8 categories, 12 unlockable items earned by quests, puzzles, NPC gifts, a hidden find and three world chests, a picture picker, a "new item" reveal, saved decor state.

## Known flake (not understood)
In 2 of 6 development runs of `v02.mjs`, one picker tap in the 12-item placement loop did not register (the wall or rug picker stayed open and the next hotspot reported "not offered"). The next 4 runs, including 3 back to back, passed. The driver now prints `tap-missed` and saves a `STILLOPEN_*` screenshot if it happens again. It could be a test-timing issue or a real input race; it is **not** confirmed either way. Please watch for a picker card that needs a second tap when testing on the iPad.

## Design notes / limits
- Letters: only the /b/ puzzle has a letter card. Other puzzles use picture targets. More letters need new puzzle entries (data only, `letter: 'M'` etc.).
- New narration lines were NOT added for the reveal or the picker (picture-only by design); the manifest is unchanged.
- Old saves keep working; their `captions` flag is preserved, `narration` becomes OFF.
- Decor art is new pixel art generated by `tools/art_props.py` in the existing style; judged only by looking at the browser screenshots.

## Bugs found by running it (all fixed)
1. A story-triggered form change (shimmer -> mermaid) was blocked by the busy flag, so the mermaid transformation never happened.
2. A stale tree sprite from the woods crashed the next scene (shore) on arrival, leaving a black screen.
3. The puzzle panel repeated a question the dialogue had just spoken.
4. Parent settings layout overflowed; grotto looked like a beach (added a multiply water tint and bubbles); stale counting tray carried between scenes.

## Narration status (updated v0.1b): temporary synthetic clips exist; final voices do not
- All 81 lines now have **temporary** `.m4a` + `.ogg` clips generated offline with CMU Flite (the `flite` filter in the local ffmpeg; no network, no real person's voice).
  Each character has its own synthetic voice/pitch. They are robotic placeholders: **I could only verify that they play, not how pleasant they sound to a 5-year-old.** Listen on the iPad.
- Provenance is in `site/assets/audio/narration/provenance.json` (`source: tts`). Files you add yourself are never overwritten by `tools/make_tts_clips.py`; `--status` shows `temp-tts` / `custom` / `missing`.
- Organised by character and scene in `docs/AUDIO_SCRIPT.md`; machine-readable `docs/narration_manifest.json`.
- Replace any clip by dropping `<key>.m4a` (+ `.ogg`) into `assets/audio/narration/` of the deployed site; no engine rebuild.

## iPad / design caveats worth knowing
- Portrait works (smaller) with a picture-only "turn sideways" hint; landscape is the intended mode.
- Dialogue captions are on by default for grown-ups; toggle in the parent menu.
- Bedroom decorating is tap-to-cycle on glowing spots (no separate decor tray).
